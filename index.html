Strickland Home

A custom home screen for Fire TV. Install the launcher first, then Home on Fire so it opens at startup and when you press Home.

1. Download Strickland HomeVersion 0.7.3: the weather icon switches to the moon at the real sunset and back to the sun at sunrise 2. Download Home on FireFree and open source, from its official GitHub page

Strickland Home for onn.
